# Portfolio Summary

Loan Origination & Credit Workflow Platform is a self-directed engineering case study showing how a lending workflow can remain explainable as product terms, policy, human review, and provider outcomes change. It demonstrates .NET 10 modular architecture, SQLite zero-infrastructure operation, declarative rules, transparent risk factors, financial calculations, and controls around decisions and disbursement.

The differentiator is the persisted decision trace: every ruleset ID/version, rule result, input read, score contribution, and product version is retained rather than hidden behind a fabricated credit score.
