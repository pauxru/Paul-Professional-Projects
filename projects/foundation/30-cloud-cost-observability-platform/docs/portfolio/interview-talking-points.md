# Interview Talking Points

## Why is this architecture non-trivial?
The hard part is trustworthy attribution under missing metadata and shared services. An ordered, auditable rule chain plus a mathematically explicit residual makes the report defensible.

## What breaks at 3am?
Provider exports arrive late, restate old days, contain unknown resources, and cost behavior is naturally seasonal. The design makes replay safe, rejects unknown inventory for recovery, records import outcomes, and avoids naïve anomaly thresholds.

## What would change in an enterprise?
OIDC/managed identities, provider export delivery, object storage, an analytical warehouse/read model, secure download controls, alert routing, approval workflows, and independently retained audit records.

## Why unit economics?
Cloud spend alone cannot distinguish waste from valuable scale. Cost per order, active tenant, or GB processed connects infrastructure changes to a business denominator without making revenue claims.
